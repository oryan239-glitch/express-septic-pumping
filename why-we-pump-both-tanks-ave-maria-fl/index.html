How to tell whether this planned town house even has two chambers

AMUC is not Collier County Public Utilities. Look at the bill. If you pay a wastewater charge to Ave Maria Utility Company, there is nothing here to pump, and a two-lid diagram does not apply. If wastewater stays on the lot, a two-compartment tank still needs both sides emptied. We clean an outlet filter when one is fitted, included in the pump-out.

Campus calendars and winter stays change the fill rate, not the compartments we open

Graduation, move-in, and holiday weeks can fill a house that sat quiet. That is a reason to schedule a complete pump-out before guests arrive, not a reason to treat the second chamber as optional. We will not invent a required January date, or a numbered cleaning interval, that is supposed to fit every seasonal Ave Maria household. The work on a two-chamber tank stays the same: inlet and outlet.

Town-center hardscape and grass on the fringe hide covers differently

Pavers, tight plantings, and compact setbacks around the town core can conceal both lids. Grass and longer yards on the edge of town usually make the tank easier to see, but the job does not shrink to one chamber because the cover is obvious. Mention landscaping or a hardscape lid when you book. Locating and exposing a buried cover is quoted on the call.

The rural grid next door is not a stand-in for this town

East of Naples, the Estates grid is mostly wells and septic, often with mound fields in low ground. Ave Maria's core is a planned utility service area. Ave Maria both-compartment pumping questions

Does every Ave Maria address have septic chambers to empty?

No. Ave Maria Utility Company provides potable water, wastewater, and reclaimed water in much of the planned town. A house that pays that wastewater charge does not have septic chambers. This page is only for properties whose wastewater still stays on the lot. Call (239) 451-3261 if you are unsure.

If the house does have a tank, do you empty both compartments?

Yes, when it is built with two chambers. An effluent filter in the outlet, if one is there, is cleaned during that same stop and is included in the pump-out. There is no invented standalone filter-only price.

Do graduation or winter guests change which lid you open?

They change how quickly the tank fills, not the work. We still empty the inlet and the outlet. We will not publish a required month or a numbered interval that is supposed to fit every seasonal or university-week household.

What does residential pumping start at here?

Residential pumping for a typical 1 to 3 bedroom tank begins at $399.99, and we will verify that figure for your tank by phone. A backup that cannot wait for morning is $699.99–$799.99 by home size after hours; call us rather than submitting the booking form.
