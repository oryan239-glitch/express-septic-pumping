Along the Caloosahatchee in Alva, household septic tanks on homes, ranches, and farms commonly sit in ground that gets wet in the rainy season, which is a reason to empty the outlet chamber, not a reason to leave it loaded. This page is about those household tanks, reached along State Road 80 and the rural roads that run off it. It is not about livestock lagoons. Why a river-adjacent wet season makes a half-empty tank a poor fit

When the river is up and the yard is soft, a drain field is already working in wet soil. Wastewater still enters the inlet first, where solids should settle and scum should float. The outlet is the last stop before that field. If a visit only pumps the inlet, leftover sludge and scum can sit in the second chamber and then move toward ground that has little extra capacity. We do not invent a count of Alva fields damaged this way. We empty both compartments on a two-chamber household tank, and we clean an effluent filter in the outlet when one is present, included in the pump-out.

Ranch lanes are access notes, not a different pumping job

A locked gate, a long lane, livestock fencing, or soft shoulders after a storm can all be part of reaching an Alva tank. Mention those details when you book so we can plan to open both covers in one visit. If a cover has to be located and dug, that work is quoted on the call. A long approach changes the clock. It does not make the second chamber optional.

Older rural tanks may not look like a catalog drawing

Some tanks on older Alva properties do not match a newer two-lid diagram. There may be one obvious cover and a second that has been buried for years, or a layout that predates a common baffle wall. Tell us what you know. We open the covers that are actually there and empty the compartments the tank has. We will not invent a layout we have not seen, and we will not treat a missing second lid as a reason to skip the outlet if that chamber exists.

Buckingham is also rural and east of Fort Myers, but it is not this river-and-ranch stretch along State Road 80. City of Fort Myers utility pages do not describe an Alva lot.
