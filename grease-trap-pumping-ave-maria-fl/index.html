Where are Ave Maria kitchens?

Most food service sits in the compact town center and along the boulevards that serve the university and the surrounding neighborhoods. Event weeks, graduation, and the winter season can fill those rooms more than a quiet summer afternoon. That is a reason to book before a due date lands in the middle of a packed stretch, not a reason to change the price. We do not list private restaurants or campus kitchens as customers. Who is the sewer utility in Ave Maria?

Ave Maria Utility Company (AMUC) provides potable water, wastewater treatment, and reclaimed water for the town. That is a different provider from Collier County Public Utilities. During this research we could not independently retrieve an official AMUC ordinance or policy text that sets a specific indoor-trap or interceptor pumping interval. Third-party frequencies will not be copied here. Confirm the current grease-control schedule with AMUC at 239-348-0248, and follow the utility that actually bills the account.

When would Collier County's FOG program apply?

The official Collier County Food Service Establishments Program publishes 30-day indoor-trap and 90-day interceptor intervals for food-service establishments in the county program on the county wastewater system. An Ave Maria kitchen on AMUC sewer should not treat those county numbers as automatic town law. If a nearby property outside the AMUC territory is actually billed by Collier County, then the county program may apply. Check the bill.

What Florida law still applies?

Under Florida Statute 403.0741, a signed grease waste service manifest belongs with each pump-out, and gray-water return into the interceptor or trap is prohibited. Chapter 62-705, F.A.C., addresses handling, storage, and disposal. We can match a repeating route to whatever interval AMUC requires once you have it. This town page does not offer Express as a manifest writer, a licensed grease hauler, or a compliance desk. If a lid is under pavers in the town center, locating and digging it is $100, plus $50 for each extra hour. After-hours pumping during an active backup is $599.99; use the phone. Household septic work around the town, where it still applies, is on septic tank pumping in Ave Maria.

How Ave Maria differs from the Estates grid next door

Golden Gate Estates is a spread of acre lots, many of them on septic. Ave Maria is a compact, planned town with a university calendar and a single utility company for typical town water and sewer accounts. That is why this page leads with AMUC and asks you to confirm AMUC's current grease schedule, and why the Estates page leads with the sewer-versus-septic question. AMUC also provides reclaimed water for irrigation in the community; that fact does not change the $695 price for a 1,000-gallon grease unit, but it is a reminder that the town treats its wastewater on a closed local system rather than sending it automatically to a county plant. Tell us about town-center parking, event closures, and lids in pavers when you book.
