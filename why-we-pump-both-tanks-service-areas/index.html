Why we pump both septic tank compartments

Call (239) 451-3261Book routine service

A septic tank with two compartments is not finished when only the first lid has been pumped. The inlet chamber is where solids settle and scum floats. The outlet chamber is the last stop before the drain field, and often before an effluent filter. We empty both sides, and we clean the filter when one is present, as part of a residential pump-out. Pick a city page below for how this applies to conversions, island lots, canal streets, or inland wells. For a standard 1 to 3 bedroom tank, residential pumping starts at $399.99. A short script for any pumper, including us

Ask two questions before the truck arrives: will you empty the outlet chamber as well as the inlet, and will you clean the effluent filter if the tank has one? Those questions do not name other companies, and they do not require you to know a percentage of incomplete pump-outs. They simply make the work specific. We answer yes to both when the tank is built that way. Filter-only city pages, which are a different topic, are listed from septic filter care and the filter service-areas hub.

Both-compartment questions

Do you empty both the inlet and the outlet on a residential pump-out?

Yes, when the tank is built with two compartments. If the tank has an effluent filter, we clean it as part of that visit, with no separate filter fee. Call (239) 451-3261, or use ServiceM8 for planned work.

What is left behind if only the inlet is pumped?

Sludge and scum can remain in the outlet chamber and then move toward the drain field, or pack the effluent filter and contribute to slow drains or a backup. A complete pump-out empties both sides.

What should I ask any pumper?

Ask whether they will open and empty both compartments, not only the inlet, and whether they will clean the filter if one is there. The phrase 'pump the tank' does not by itself answer either question.

What does residential pumping start at?

Residential pumping starts at $399.99 for a standard 1 to 3 bedroom tank. We confirm the price for your tank when you call. After-hours pumping during an active backup is $699.99–$799.99 by home size.
