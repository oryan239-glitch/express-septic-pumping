Who actually has a filter here, and who does not?

Look at the utility bill rather than assuming every Immokalee mailing address is on a tank. A house on Immokalee Water & Sewer District sewer is not the audience for this page. A lot whose wastewater stays on the property likely has a tank; we will tell you whether that tank was built with an outlet filter when we open it. Collier County Public Utilities is a different provider again, and it is not automatic here. If the property is actually closer to the Estates grid or to Ave Maria, use those city pages.

What a clogged outlet does on a farm or rural lot

When the screen packs up, wastewater can turn back toward house fixtures even if the tank still has capacity. That is the 'unexpected' backup: a toilet or a laundry sink that worked yesterday, then does not. If that screen is absent or broken, solids can head toward the drain field. On a lot that also has a well or agricultural buildings, that field still has to do its job. Cleaning the filter will not repair a field that has already failed, and it will not replace pumping. It will take away a common, avoidable obstruction.

Access on agricultural and rural parcels

Look under the outlet cover, at the end of the tank wastewater leaves from, to reach the screen. On a compact in-town lot that may be a short walk from the driveway. On a farm or rural parcel it can be a long hose run past a gate or fencing. Mention that when you book, including soft ground after rain. A buried cover that must be found and dug is $100 to start, then $50 for each added hour. If you want the lid at the surface so a later check does not start with a shovel, a 6-inch riser is $299.99 each.

Immokalee filter cleaning questions

Are Immokalee houses on Immokalee Water & Sewer District, or on a septic tank?

It depends on the address. Immokalee Water & Sewer District bills many properties in town. A house on that sewer system would not have an effluent filter. Rural homes, farms, and businesses around Immokalee often still depend on a septic tank; we will tell you if that tank has an outlet filter when we open it. Check the bill, then call (239) 451-3261.

Is filter cleaning included when you pump an Immokalee-area tank?

Yes, when a filter is present. We include it in the pump-out and can demonstrate how it seats if you want to be able to check it yourself later.

How do I know it is time to look at the filter?

Have it cleaned when we empty the tank. If sinks or a shower start to drain slowly before the next pump-out, look at it then. We do not give Immokalee a numbered weekly or monthly filter schedule.

What if the lid is out on a farm or rural lot?

Tell us about a gate, a long driveway, or livestock fencing when you book. If the lid has to be located and uncovered, that work starts at $100, then $50 for each extra hour. If you want that lid at grade, a 6-inch riser is priced at $299.99 each.
