1. Program Name and Description

Program name: Express Septic Tank Pumping Customer Notifications

Express Septic Tank Pumping LLC ("we," "us," "our") sends text messages to customers who have opted in, about septic tank pumping, grease trap pumping, and related local services. Messages are sent from our business number, (239) 451-3261.

2. Types of Messages

By opting in, you may receive the following types of text messages:

  • Appointment reminders (for example, a reminder of your scheduled septic pumping date and time);
  • Scheduling confirmations (confirming, rescheduling, or updating a service appointment, including technician arrival updates);
  • Missed-call follow-ups (a text after we miss your call, so we can help with your request); and
  • Customer service replies (answers to your questions about quotes, service, invoices, and your account).

Sample message: "Express Septic Tank Pumping: Your septic pumping appointment is confirmed for Tue 10/13 between 8-10 AM. Reply C to confirm or call (239) 451-3261. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help."

3. How You Opt In

You may opt in to receive text messages by:

  • Text BOOK: texting BOOK to (239) 451-3261 to get our online booking link, as shown on our website;
  • Phone call: verbally agreeing, during a phone call with us, to receive text messages when you provide your mobile number or ask us to text you; or
  • Website form: checking the optional SMS consent box (unchecked by default) on the quote/request form at https://expresssepticpumping.com/ or https://expresssepticpumping.com/contact/, agreeing to receive text messages from Express Septic Tank Pumping LLC at the number you provide about your quote, appointment scheduling, arrival times, and service updates.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchase. You may also contact us by phone at (239) 451-3261 or by email at expressseptictankpumping@gmail.com instead.

4. Message Frequency and Cost

Message frequency may vary. Frequency depends on your service activity, such as appointments and service requests.

Message and data rates may apply. Check your mobile plan for details.

5. Opting Out (STOP)

To opt out at any time, text STOP. You may also reply END, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE, QUIT, or STOPALL. After you text STOP, you will receive one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and you will no longer receive text messages from us unless you opt in again. To rejoin, text START or tell us you would like to receive texts again.

6. Help (HELP)

For assistance, text HELP or visit https://expresssepticpumping.com/ / call (239) 451-3261. You may also email expressseptictankpumping@gmail.com.

7. Privacy and Data Sharing

SMS consent is not shared with third parties. Mobile opt-in data will not be shared with third parties. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

See our Privacy Policy for more information: https://expresssepticpumping.com/privacy-policy

You confirm that you are the account holder or authorized user of the mobile number you provide. If you change or give up your mobile number, please text STOP from that number or tell us so we can update our records.
