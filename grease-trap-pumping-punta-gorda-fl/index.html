Where are Punta Gorda kitchens concentrated?

Downtown and the historic blocks near the harbor draw evening and weekend visitors. U.S. 41 carries a longer commercial strip. The waterfront and event calendar change how full a trap can get between visits, especially in winter. We do not name private restaurants or the Village as customers. Who regulates FOG on City of Punta Gorda sewer?

The official City of Punta Gorda FOG Program describes the city's fats, oil, and grease program. The city's Wastewater Collection division includes a Pretreatment Coordinator responsible for monthly FOG inspections of local restaurants and fast-food establishments, and for code-compliance issues related to the city's pretreatment ordinance. The city also publishes grease-interceptor design guidance and an approved-haulers list. During this research the city's FOG page returned an access-denied response from this environment, and we did not independently retrieve a primary ordinance text we could quote for a required pumping frequency such as every 30 days or every 90 days. We will not copy Charlotte County Utilities' 30- and 90-day intervals onto a city-sewer account. Confirm the current indoor-trap and interceptor schedule with City of Punta Gorda Water and Wastewater / Pretreatment at 941-575-5078.

When would Charlotte County Utilities' 30- and 90-day rules apply?

The official Charlotte County commercial FOG practices flyer and Charlotte County FOG best-management guide state that grease traps must be pumped at least every 30 days and that every interceptor shall be cleaned every 90 days or sooner, with a possible exemption from the 90-day minimum if the user can establish that the schedule is not necessary. Those documents belong to Charlotte County Utilities' FOG program for county sewer customers. They apply if the Punta Gorda-area kitchen is actually billed by the county, not as automatic city law. Check the utility bill.

What Florida law still applies?

Florida Statute 403.0741 requires a signed grease waste service manifest at the pump-out and prohibits gray-water return into the interceptor or trap.
