How often does a grease interceptor need pumping in Lee County?

Lee County Ordinance 14-24 requires grease interceptors to be fully pumped at least every 90 calendar days. Indoor grease traps must be cleaned at least weekly or whenever grease and solids exceed 20% of the unit volume. We can set up a recurring pump-out schedule that fits your kitchen.

What Florida law says about grease waste manifests

Florida Statute 403.0741 requires a signed grease waste service manifest for every pump-out.
