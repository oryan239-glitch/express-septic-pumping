Southwest Florida septic maintenance checklist

This is a task list, not another explanation of how often most tanks are pumped. The how often to pump page already covers the U.S. EPA SepticSmart 3 to 5 year range and the factors that shorten it. The maintenance tips page covers what not to flush. Use this checklist to plan the year in a climate with a rainy season, holiday guests, and houses that sit part of the year.

What should I do before the summer rainy season?

If you are due for pumping, or you cannot remember the last date, schedule it before the water table rises. A tank with room in it gives the drain field a better chance when the yard is already wet. Residential pumping starts at $399.99 (standard 1–3 bed). Walk the field after a typical rain later in the season so you know what 'normal wet' looks like for your lot.

What should I do before guests, holidays, or snowbird return?

Check the last pump-out date. If a full house is about to arrive and the tank is overdue, pump first. If you are coming back after months away, do the same before you run laundry and showers all day. The snowbird page is written for that return; this checklist is the reminder to put it on the calendar.

What is worth a monthly walk-around?

Odors, gurgling, slow drains, soggy spots, and grass that is much greener over the drain field than the rest of the yard. You are not opening lids every month. You are noticing change. If something looks wrong, call (239) 451-3261 rather than adding more water to the system.

What belongs on the annual service visit?

A pump-out when it is due, filter cleaning (included at no charge when Express pumps), and a conversation about access. If the lid is still buried, consider a riser at $299.99 each so the next visit is faster. If you want a written look at the system as well, an inspection with pump-out is $549.99.

Where do I go for habits, not dates?

For what never to flush, water use, and drain field care, stay with the maintenance guide. For bacteria, bleach, and additives, use good bacteria and the drain field. This page stays on timing.

Maintenance checklist questions

Is this the same as the how-often-to-pump page?

No. That page explains the 3 to 5 year range and the factors that change it. This checklist is a season-by-season list of tasks for Southwest Florida homes. Use both.

What should I do before the rainy season?

If a pump-out is due, schedule it before summer rains raise the water table. Walk the drain field after a storm later, but do not wait for standing water to think about a full tank.

What belongs on a monthly once-over?

Look and listen: odors, slow drains, soggy ground, unusually green grass over the field. You do not need to open the tank every month.

When should the effluent filter be cleaned?

At every pump-out and whenever drains slow. Express cleans it at no charge when we pump. There is no separate monthly interval that fits every house.
