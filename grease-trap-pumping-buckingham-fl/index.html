Which rule applies: Ordinance 14-24 or no sewer program?

Buckingham does not have its own municipal utility. If a kitchen discharges to Lee County Utilities, Lee County Ordinance 14-24 applies: interceptors fully pumped at least every 90 calendar days, and indoor grease traps cleaned at least weekly or whenever grease and solids exceed 20% of the unit volume. Large-lot Buckingham properties typically use septic tanks. For a kitchen on a septic system, there is no LCU FOG calendar to paste onto the interceptor.
