Septic guide for Realtors

Call (239) 451-3261Book routine service

If you list or sell homes on septic in Southwest Florida, your clients often need a clear next step more than a lecture on tanks. This page is written for agents. It covers when to suggest a pump-out or inspection, how to help buyers who have only known city sewer, and how to reach Express without slowing a contract. It is not legal advice about disclosure; your broker and the parties' attorneys handle that.

When should I recommend a septic inspection?

Recommend one when the property has a septic tank and the file has no recent pumping record, the buyers are uncertain, or the listing is in an area where many lots are still on tanks rather than central sewer. A septic inspection with pump-out is $549.99. That visit is the cleanest way for both sides to see the tank, not a substitute for a general home inspection.

How do I talk about septic with first-time buyers?

Many buyers moving to Estero, Bonita Springs, or unincorporated Lee and Collier County are leaving municipal sewer for the first time. Send them to the first-time owner guide so they can read about lids, filters, and drain fields at their own pace. On a showing, it is enough to say the home treats wastewater on site, records should be requested, and Express can inspect and pump if they want a current look.

What belongs in the file besides the marketing remarks?

Ask the seller for the last pumping date, any risers already installed, and whether the lids are buried. If the lid is buried, locating and digging it is $100, plus $50 for each additional hour, which is useful to mention so a buyer is not surprised on service day. A riser at $299.99 each can be a simple improvement to note in negotiations. For a longer checklist aimed at the parties themselves, use buying or selling a home with a septic tank.

How do I schedule around a closing date?

Call (239) 451-3261 and give us the inspection deadline or closing date. For routine pumping or a planned inspection, clients can also request a time on ServiceM8. We contact them to confirm; a request is not a booked appointment until we confirm. If a occupied house has a backup, they should call, not book online.

Where do you work?

Express is based in Estero and serves Lee, Collier, and Charlotte County, plus LaBelle. We pump tanks for listings and purchases across those areas, including Fort Myers, Cape Coral, Naples, Lehigh Acres, and the islands. We do not quote drain field replacement or repairs until we inspect the system.

Realtor septic guide questions

When should a listing mention a septic inspection?

If the home is on a septic tank and the last pump-out or inspection is unknown, recommend a septic inspection with pump-out before or during the inspection period. That service is $549.99. Call (239) 451-3261.

Can Express work on a short closing timeline?

Call and tell us the contract dates. Routine work can often be requested through ServiceM8; a backup is not something to book online. We confirm times after we hear from you.

What should I tell buyers who have only lived on city sewer?

Point them to our first-time septic guide and to a professional inspection, not to guesses about the tank's condition.

Do you provide a report I can share with both sides?

After an inspection with pump-out, we tell you what we saw. Share that conversation and any written notes with your clients and their inspectors as you normally would for other property reports.
