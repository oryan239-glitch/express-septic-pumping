Why we pump both septic tank compartments in Lehigh Acres, FL

Most Lehigh Acres houses still have a septic tank, often with a well on the same lot, so a complete pump-out of both chambers is ordinary maintenance here, not a special request. The community is large, flat, and mostly inland in eastern Lee County. Older houses, including many built from the 1960s through the early 1980s, may not match a newer two-lid, filtered diagram. We open what is there, empty both compartments when the tank is built that way, and clean the filter if one is present. Call (239) 451-3261 or request a Lehigh Acres pump-out on ServiceM8.

The well and the outlet chamber belong in the same conversation

On a lot that drinks from a private well and treats its own wastewater, the drain field is working in the same ground. The inlet chamber should hold most solids. The outlet chamber should be sending clearer water to that field. If a pump-out empties only the inlet, leftover sludge and scum in the outlet can move toward the field, or pack a filter and send wastewater back into the house. We do not invent a contamination figure. We do empty both chambers because the field and the well have little room for a shortcut.

Older Lehigh tanks may not match the diagram you looked up

Some tanks from those earlier decades were built with one compartment, or without an effluent filter. Others have two chambers and a filter in the outlet. We will tell you what we find rather than force a newer diagram onto an older tank. If there is no filter, emptying the outlet is even more important, because there is no screen to catch solids that a skipped second chamber would otherwise release. If there is a filter, we clean it during the pump-out, included in the visit.

Finding both lids on the Lehigh grid

The grid can hide lids under grass, fill, or a long side yard. A complete pump-out needs both access points when there are two compartments. Tell us if you only know one spot, or if a prior visit seemed to use a single lid. Locating and uncovering a buried lid is quoted on the call. In the rainy season the water table comes up on this flat ground; that is another reason not to leave the outlet loaded ahead of summer rain.

What to ask any pumper, and what we include

Ask whether both chambers will be opened and emptied, not only the first lid, and whether a filter will be cleaned if one is there. We do that work on the visit.
