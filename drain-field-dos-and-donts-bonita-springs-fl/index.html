Drain field do's and don'ts in Bonita Springs, FL

Call (239) 451-3261Book routine service

On a Bonita Springs lot that still uses a drain field, especially east of I-75 or in an older neighborhood not yet connected to Bonita Springs Utilities sewer, what you pour, flush, plant, and park on the yard is what that field has to accept through a wet summer. A conversion project in the area does not retire those habits early. This page is for houses that still treat wastewater on the lot. Call (239) 451-3261 or request a Bonita Springs pump-out on ServiceM8.

Why does a Bonita kitchen sink matter to the field out back?

Cooking oil, fats, and grease cooled in a can belong in the trash, not down the drain. In the tank they float as scum; if they leave the tank they coat soil and slow the field. We do not invent a count of Bonita fields damaged by kitchen grease. We do treat grease as a household don't, year-round and in seasonal houses that sit quiet and then cook heavily in winter.

Are wipes labeled flushable any safer while a street waits on BSU sewer?

No. Wet wipes, even packages marked flushable, do not belong in a Bonita toilet. They do not break down the way toilet paper does. They can mat in the tank, load an outlet, and send solids toward the field. Use biodegradable, septic-safe toilet paper, and put wipes in the trash. A coming sewer connection does not make those wipes acceptable in the months or years the tank is still in service.

How do bleach and harsh cleaners affect the soil side of a Bonita system?

The field depends on bacteria in the tank and in the soil to treat effluent. Large amounts of bleach, drain opener, or heavy disinfectant can knock that process back. Ordinary cleaning of a bathroom is not the issue; pouring leftover cleaner down the drain is. More on that process is on how soil and tank bacteria protect a drain field.

What should never sit on the ground above a Bonita drain field?

Do not park or drive on it, even for a weekend project or a guest's extra car. Do not put a shed, a pool, a spa, or a patio on that footprint. Keep trees and deep-rooted shrubs off the field; turf is the cover that belongs there. Roof gutters and irrigation should be aimed away from the laterals so the soil is not kept soaked on purpose. Bonita's rainy season already raises the water table; extra water from a downspout or a sprinkler zone on top of the field is a poor addition.

How should laundry and a disposal be used in older or seasonal Bonita houses?

Spread laundry through the week instead of stacking many loads on one day, which can push water and solids toward the field before they settle. A garbage disposal adds more solids; it is not banned here, but heavy daily use is a reason to stay current on pumping rather than stretch the interval. Seasonal arrivals should not treat the first week back as a time to run every appliance at once if the tank has not been opened in a long while.

A short note for Bonita restaurants, and the prices that belong on this page

Food-service kitchens are a different system than a house tank. Where Bonita Springs Utilities' FOG program applies, interceptors are pumped on that utility's schedule, including a 90-day interceptor interval and a 25% wetted-height rule that BSU publishes. Lee County Ordinance 14-24 applies to kitchens discharging to Lee County Utilities, not automatically to every Bonita address; indoor traps under 14-24 are cleaned at least weekly or whenever grease and solids exceed 20% of unit volume, which is not a rule for interceptors. Bonita Springs drain field do's and don'ts questions

Does a Bonita house still on a tank need these drain field habits if sewer is coming?

Yes, until the house is actually connected and the tank is abandoned. A conversion announcement does not make wipes, grease, or parking on the field safe in the meantime. Call (239) 451-3261.

What toilet paper should I use on a Bonita septic system?

Use biodegradable, septic-safe toilet paper. Do not flush wet wipes, even ones labeled flushable. Those belong in the trash.

Do bleach and heavy cleaners hurt the drain field?

Large amounts of bleach and harsh cleaners can kill the bacteria that treat waste in the tank and in the drain-field soil. See our page on why good bacteria protect the septic tank and drain field. Normal household cleaning is not the same as pouring disinfectant down the drains.
