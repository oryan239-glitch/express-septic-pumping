Drain field do's and don'ts on Fort Myers Beach, FL

On a low, sandy Fort Myers Beach lot that still has a drain field, leftover grease, a parked car on the laterals, or a week of rental-turnover laundry can ask more of the soil than the yard has elevation to spare, and not every house on Estero Boulevard still has a field. Why kitchen grease is a poor match for a low beach field

Oil and fats poured down a sink collect in the tank and can move toward a field that already sits close to the water table. Keep them out of the drain. We do not invent a count of beach fields damaged this way. We do treat grease as a don't, in year-round houses and in rentals that turn over every week.

What should guests never put in a beach-house toilet?

Wet wipes, including flushable packaging, go in the trash. Use biodegradable, septic-safe toilet paper. A short note in a rental bathroom does more than hoping visitors read a wrapper. Paper towels and feminine products belong in the trash as well.

Cleaners after turnover, and the bacteria the field needs

Turnover cleaning is not a reason to empty leftover bleach or drain opener into the system. Large amounts of those products can set back the bacteria in the tank and in the drain-field soil. See why those organisms matter to a drain field.

Parking, sand, pools, and runoff on a low lot

Do not park or drive on the field, even if sand has shifted and the footprint is no longer obvious. Do not add a pool, a spa, or a structure on the laterals. Keep deep roots off that ground; turf is the right cover. Aim roof water and sprinklers elsewhere so a storm is not soaking soil that already has little freeboard.

Laundry at changeover, and staying current on pumping

Spread laundry out rather than running every load on turnover day. A disposal adds solids; use it lightly. Fort Myers Beach drain field do's and don'ts questions

Does every house on Estero Boulevard have a drain field?

No. Some properties are on sewer and would not have a household field. Confirm the bill before you follow this checklist. Call (239) 451-3261.

Why is a low, sandy beach lot sensitive to extra water and parking?

The field already sits in low, sandy ground. A vehicle on that footprint compacts the soil, and extra water from gutters, irrigation, or a stack of laundry leaves even less room. Keep vehicles off the laterals and spread water use out.

Are flushable wipes acceptable in a rental bathroom?

No. Wipes, even flushable labels, belong in the trash. Use biodegradable, septic-safe toilet paper, and put a short note where guests will see it.
