Which FOG rules apply in Bonita Springs?

Many Bonita Springs food-service accounts discharge to Bonita Springs Utilities. BSU's franchise area includes the City of Bonita Springs, a portion of the Village of Estero, and unincorporated south Lee County; see the official BSU franchise service area. For those BSU sewer customers, the Bonita Springs Utilities Sewer Use Policy (BOD approved August 2019) is the document to read.

Section 7 of that policy requires grease interceptors to be completely evacuated at least every 90 days, or more often if 25 percent or more of the wetted height of the interceptor, measured from the bottom of the device to the invert of the outlet pipe, contains floating materials, sediment, or FOG; if discharge limits are exceeded; or if there is a history of non-compliance. Cleaning means complete removal of grease, water, and solids. Grease waste may not be recirculated or disposed of into the BSU wastewater system, and 'pump and return' pump-outs are prohibited. The BSU grease interceptor maintenance card also states a scheduled minimum 90-day pumping and cleaning frequency and tells facilities to send pump-out manifests or receipts to BSU within 30 days. That is the facility's record-keeping obligation. This page does not claim that Express provides manifests, is a licensed grease hauler, or handles compliance reporting.

Lee County Ordinance 14-24 is written for food-service establishments that discharge to Lee County Utilities, not as a substitute for the BSU policy on a BSU account. If a Bonita kitchen is somehow billed by a different utility, confirm that provider before you assume either schedule.

What Florida law still applies on top of BSU's policy?

Florida Statute 403.0741 requires a signed grease waste service manifest for every pump-out and prohibits gray-water return practices, including discharging removed grease waste back into an interceptor or trap. Chapter 62-705 of the Florida Administrative Code addresses handling, storage, and disposal of FOG. We cite those rules so kitchens know they exist; we do not use this page to claim hauler licensing or reporting work.

After-hours pumping is $699.99–$799.99 by home size if a backup is already happening; call rather than using the online form during an overflow.

How is this different from septic pumping at a Bonita home?

Grease equipment is not a household septic tank. Our truck uses a split 4,200-gallon tank so grease and septic loads do not mix. Home septic pumping in the city is covered on septic tank pumping in Bonita Springs. Food trucks that use a Bonita commissary can also see food trucks and commissary kitchens.
