Household size near campus can change the clock, not the chambers

When students, relatives, or extra occupants are in the house during the school year, a tank that seemed fine in May can be overdue by October. That swing is a reason to book a full pump-out, not a reason to leave the outlet loaded. Wastewater still enters the inlet first, solids and scum still belong in that first chamber, and the outlet is still the last stop before the drain field. We pump both compartments on a two-chamber tank, and we clean an effluent filter in the outlet when one is there, included in the pump-out.

Sewer and septic can sit on neighboring streets

San Carlos Park is close enough to other Lee County utility service areas that you should not assume the house still has a tank. Look at the bill. A sewer charge means there are no chambers to empty, and this page does not apply. A house that still uses a tank needs both compartments pumped until that tank is abandoned for sewer. We will not invent a street-by-street conversion map; the bill is the reliable check.

Tight yards are why we ask about both covers up front

Lots here are smaller than the acre parcels east of town. A fence, a shed, a pool cage, or plantings can put the second lid in a corner you cannot see from the driveway. Tell us what the yard looks like when you book. If a cover has to be found and exposed, that locating work is $100, then $50 for each additional hour. Compact access changes how we set up, not whether the outlet gets emptied.

San Carlos Park both-compartment pumping questions

Does every San Carlos Park street still have a septic tank?

No. Some streets have been connected to sewer, and those houses would not have chambers to empty. Confirm the utility bill before you schedule a pump-out. Call (239) 451-3261 if you want help sorting it out.

If the house is on a tank, will you empty both compartments?

Yes, when the tank is built with two chambers. A filter in the outlet, when there is one, is cleaned during that visit with no separate filter charge.

School-year crowding near FGCU changes the work, right?

It can change how fast the tank fills when students or extra household members are home. It does not change which lids we open. We still empty the inlet and the outlet.
