FHA, VA, and lender septic requirements

If you are buying or refinancing a Southwest Florida home on a septic tank with an FHA loan, a VA loan, or another mortgage, the lender and the appraiser decide what the file needs, not the pumping company. This page summarizes official starting points and explains the visit we can provide when a party asks for one. It is not legal advice, it is not an underwriting opinion, and it does not promise that any particular report will clear a loan condition. A septic inspection with pump-out is $549.99. What does official FHA guidance say about onsite sewage systems?

HUD's Single Family Housing Policy Handbook 4000.1 addresses onsite sewage disposal systems. FHA Mortgagee Letter 2025-18, dated June 27, 2025, restates the appraiser's required analysis and reporting on that subject and is published by HUD at hud.gov. The handbook itself is available from HUD's Handbook 4000.1 page.

In that Mortgagee Letter, HUD describes an onsite sewage disposal system as a wastewater system designed to treat and dispose of effluent on the same property that produces the wastewater. The appraiser must notify the mortgagee of an MPR or MPS deficiency if the property is not on an off-site sewer system and a living unit is not provided with an onsite system adequate to dispose of all domestic wastes in a manner that will not create a nuisance or endanger the public health. The appraiser must visually observe the system and its surrounding area, must require an inspection to ensure the system is in proper working order if there are readily observable signs of system failure, and must report on the availability of public sewer to the site. The appraiser must also notify the mortgagee if there is evidence that the system is not sufficient.

That is the appraiser's official starting point, not a rule that every FHA loan automatically includes a pump-out from us. The mortgagee and the appraiser decide what happens next on a given property.

What does official VA guidance say?

The Department of Veterans Affairs sets Minimum Property Requirements in the VA Lenders Handbook, VA Pamphlet 26-7, Chapter 12. VA's handbook landing page is benefits.va.gov/WARMS/pam26_7.asp. Chapter 12 includes requirements for a safe method of sewage disposal and for individual sewage disposal systems. VA has also noted that Chapter 12 was updated effective May 1, 2026, so anyone underwriting or appraising a current file should read the current handbook text rather than an old summary, including this one.

We will not invent a checklist of tank sizes, setback distances, or mandatory pump-out intervals and call them current VA rules. The VA-assigned appraiser and the lender decide whether a property meets the MPRs and whether a further inspection or a repair is required. If they ask the parties to have the tank opened or pumped, we can do that work. We cannot certify that the property 'meets VA' in a way that binds the Department.

Read the condition, ask the lender what documentation they want, and then call us if they want the tank pumped or inspected. Bring the deadline. What this page will not tell you?

We will not guess whether your particular street must hook up to public sewer, whether a 1970s tank is automatically ineligible, or whether a letter on our letterhead will clear underwriting. Official HUD and VA sources, plus your lender, are the authorities. We pump tanks in Lee, Collier, and Charlotte County, plus LaBelle, and we will be plain about what we observed once the lid is open.

FHA, VA, and lender requirements questions

Does FHA or VA always require a septic pump-out?

Not automatically. The lender, the appraiser, and the underwriter decide what a particular loan needs. Some files ask for an inspection or a pump-out; others do not. Call (239) 451-3261 if a party to the loan has asked for a visit.

Can Express certify that a home meets FHA or VA rules?

No. We pump the tank and, on an inspection with pump-out ($549.99), tell you what we saw. Loan eligibility is not our decision.

What official rules should I actually read?

For FHA, start with HUD Handbook 4000.1 and Mortgagee Letter 2025-18 on onsite sewage disposal systems. For VA, start with the VA Lenders Handbook, VA Pamphlet 26-7, Chapter 12, Minimum Property Requirements. Links are in the article above.

The closing is soon and the lid is buried. What now?

Tell us the deadline when you call. Locating and digging a buried lid is quoted on the call. Buyers and sellers can also use buying or selling a home with a septic tank.
