On Sanibel, start by confirming the house still has a tank: the city operates sanitary sewer, a single causeway shapes access, and many island addresses would not have a household field to protect. This page is only for a remaining system on a low, sandy lot, including during winter occupancy. It is not the Fort Myers Beach habit page, and it is not a stand-in for Captiva, across the Blind Pass Bridge. Call (239) 451-3261 or request a Sanibel visit on ServiceM8.

After you confirm a tank is still there, why is island grease a problem for the field?

A house on city sewer has no household laterals to protect. A house that still uses a tank should keep cooking oil and fats out of the sink. On a low island lot, leftover grease moving toward the field is a poor addition to soil that already has little unused depth. We do not invent a Sanibel failure count. We do treat kitchen grease as a don't on every remaining island field.

What should guests never flush on a Sanibel tank?

A remaining Sanibel toilet is not the place for wipes, including those whose label says flushable. Use paper meant to break down in a septic tank. Winter guests and short-term visitors who live on sewer the rest of the year may not think about that; a short note in the bath is worth more than a late-night backup, especially when the causeway is the way off the island.

Cleaners after a winter stay, and the bacteria the laterals need

A deep clean at the end of a stay is ordinary. Dumping leftover bleach or a harsh cleaner down a drain is not, because those products can interrupt the organisms a coastal field still depends on. See the bacteria a coastal field still depends on.

Cars, vegetation, and runoff on a low island lot

Do not park or drive on the laterals, and do not treat that strip as overflow parking during season. A pool, a spa, or another structure should not be built on the field. Keep deep roots off that footprint; turf is the better cover on a tight island yard. Aim roof drains and sprinklers elsewhere so stormwater is not sitting on the same sand that has to accept effluent.

Laundry after a stay, and keeping the pump-out current

When a house turns over, space washing rather than running every load at once. A disposal adds solids on top of that pulse. Island restaurants, and why we do not invent a city FOG number

Kitchens on City of Sanibel sewer follow the city's program, not a copied Lee County Utilities calendar. The city publishes Fat Free Sewers guidance, but we did not independently verify an official pumping frequency such as 30 or 90 days, so this page does not invent one. Confirm the current schedule with City of Sanibel Utilities. Sanibel drain field do's and don'ts questions

Does every Sanibel house have a drain field?

No. The City of Sanibel operates sanitary sewer, and many island addresses are on that system. Confirm the bill before you follow this page. Call (239) 451-3261.

Is this the Fort Myers Beach or Captiva habit page?

No. Sanibel is its own city, reached by the causeway, and Captiva is the next island north across the Blind Pass Bridge. Use this page only for a Sanibel address that still has a tank.

Can I copy a 30-day or 90-day grease interval onto a city kitchen?

Not from this page. The city publishes Fat Free Sewers guidance, but we did not independently verify an official city pumping frequency. Confirm the current schedule with City of Sanibel Utilities.
