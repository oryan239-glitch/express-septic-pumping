Septic filter cleaning in Bonita Springs, FL

A slow tub in an older Bonita Springs house is not always a tank that is due to be pumped. Often the effluent filter on the outlet side is clogged, and wastewater has nowhere easy to go. This page is for homeowners in Bonita Springs whose tank still has that filter, including in older neighborhoods west of I-75 and on larger lots east of the interstate. We clean the filter at no charge when we pump the tank, and we will show you how to keep it clear. Call (239) 451-3261 or request a Bonita Springs visit on ServiceM8.

What does the effluent filter actually do in a Bonita tank?

The filter sits in the outlet baffle, under the outlet lid, and catches solids before they leave the tank. If it stays open, clearer wastewater can move to the drain field. If it clogs, the tank can back up toward fixtures in the house even when the tank is not completely full. That is why a backup can feel sudden: the house worked yesterday, then a load of laundry or a weekend of guests pushes the filter past what it can pass. We do not invent a percentage of Bonita backups caused by filters. We do see this pattern often enough that checking the filter is part of every pump-out.

Why does this catch seasonal and year-round homes off guard?

Some Bonita houses sit quieter in summer and fill up in winter. A filter that was acceptable in September can slow a sink in January after weeks of heavier use. Year-round households see the same thing after holidays or when the washing machine runs back-to-back. The practical rule is simple and is not a numbered interval: clean the filter when the tank is pumped, and clean it again if drains slow before the next pump-out is due. If wastewater is already coming back into a shower, stop using water and call; after-hours pumping is $599.99.

How do you reach the filter on a Bonita lot?

The filter is under the outlet lid, not under the inlet lid at the house side of the tank. In older parts of the city the lids may be buried in a lawn. East of I-75, larger lots can hide them farther from the house, sometimes past a hedge or under mulch. If we have to locate and dig a buried lid, that work is $100, then $50 for each additional hour. Marking the lid, or telling us where it was last opened, saves time. Our shop is just north in Estero, at 23357 Olde Meadowbrook Circle, so a planned midweek visit is straightforward to schedule.

Why add a riser if you want to check the filter yourself?

A 6-inch riser is $299.99 each and brings that lid to ground level. On a Bonita lawn that gets mowed year-round, a grade-level lid is easier to find and easier to open for a filter check without digging. How many risers you need depends on how many openings the tank has; ask when you book if you want them installed while the lid is already uncovered during a pump-out.

What if Bonita Springs Utilities is bringing sewer to the neighborhood?

In some neighborhoods the City and Bonita Springs Utilities are converting streets from septic to sewer, including work described on our Bonita Springs septic pumping page, partly to reduce nutrients reaching groundwater and the Imperial River. Until your house is connected, the tank and its filter are still in service. Once sewer is available, connection is required within one year, and BSU handles that connection. Filter care does not replace that process, and it does not become optional the day a project is announced. For the general how-to, see septic filter care. Bonita Springs filter cleaning questions

Do you clean the effluent filter when you pump a Bonita Springs tank?

Yes. The outlet filter is cleaned at no extra charge as part of the pump-out, and we can show you how to keep it clear between visits. Call (239) 451-3261.

When should a Bonita homeowner check the filter?

Have it cleaned at the pump-out, and again any time sinks or toilets start to drain slowly. We do not publish a calendar interval that fits every house.

How much is a riser so I can reach the filter?

A 6-inch riser is $299.99 each. It brings that lid up to grade so later filter checks do not start with a shovel.

My street may get BSU sewer. Do I still need the filter cleaned?

Yes, until the house is actually connected. The tank and filter keep doing the work until then. After sewer is available, Bonita Springs Utilities requires connection within one year; they handle that process.
