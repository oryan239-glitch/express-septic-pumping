What does Ordinance 14-24 require here?

If the kitchen discharges to Lee County Utilities, Lee County Ordinance 14-24 requires grease interceptors to be fully pumped at least every 90 calendar days. Indoor grease traps must be cleaned at least weekly or whenever grease and solids exceed 20% of the unit volume. The 20% rule attaches to indoor traps, not to interceptors. That is the local schedule this page can cite with confidence for typical LCU accounts in unincorporated Lehigh Acres. If a kitchen is on a package plant or another provider, confirm that provider instead of assuming 14-24.

What Florida law still applies?

Florida Statute 403.0741 still applies inland: a signed grease waste service manifest must go with the pump-out, and grease waste that has been removed may not be returned to the interceptor or trap. We can line up a repeating Lehigh route with the 90-day interceptor clock, or with a tighter indoor-trap clock when that is what 14-24 requires. We are not advertising manifest issuance, grease-hauler licensing, or compliance reporting on this page.
