Express Septic Tank Pumping

Septic pumping for golf courses, marinas, and campgrounds

Call (239) 451-3261Book routine service

Golf course clubhouses, marina restrooms, and campgrounds send a burst of wastewater through a septic tank on weekends, tournament days, and seasonal holidays, then sit quieter midweek. This page is for grounds superintendents, marina managers, and campground operators who need those tanks on a schedule that matches the calendar, not a household 3 to 5 year rule of thumb. Churches, schools, and RV parks have a separate page at commercial septic for churches, schools, and RV parks. Call (239) 451-3261 with the property type and how you use it.

Why do these facilities need a seasonal plan?

A clubhouse kitchen and locker rooms can be quiet on a Tuesday and packed after a Saturday shotgun start. A marina restroom fills when boats are in and the restaurant is serving. A campground can be nearly empty in September and full in February. The tank does not get a larger capacity because the parking lot is full. A recurring pump-out timed before known peaks is more useful than waiting for an odor at the 18th green or the dock. Commercial pumping is quoted for the tank and its use. The published residential starting price of $399.99 applies to a standard 1–3 bedroom home, not automatically to these systems.

What about kitchens, dump stations, and boat facilities?

A clubhouse or marina kitchen may have a grease trap or interceptor that should be pumped on its own schedule. A 1,000-gallon unit is $695; other sizes are quoted. That work is described on grease trap and interceptor pumping. If a campground has an RV dump station tied to a tank, tell us; that kind of load changes how often the tank needs to be emptied, and we will talk about it when you call rather than inventing a frequency here. We pump septic tanks; we do not treat this page as a bilge or marina-waste service beyond the building's sanitary system.

How should a manager plan access on a large property?

Courses and camps often have long internal roads, soft shoulders after rain, and lids hidden in landscaping around a clubhouse. Tell us about cart-path only areas, locked service gates, and whether a second entrance is better for the truck. Locating and digging a buried lid is $100, plus $50 for each additional hour. A riser at $299.99 each is often worth it on a site that will be pumped on a route. For very large parcels, the access notes on acreage, ranches, and farms may also help, even though that page is written for household tanks on rural lots.

What should staff do during a weekend backup?

Close the affected restrooms if you can, stop adding water, and call (239) 451-3261. After-hours pumping is $599.99. Do not use the online booking form during an overflow. We pump both compartments when we pump and clean an effluent filter at no charge if the tank has one. If a tournament is the next morning, say so when you call. Drain field work, if the field is the problem, is quoted after we inspect the system. Waterfront or canal-side clubhouses should also watch for the patterns on waterfront and canal homes.

Can we book routine service ahead of a season?

Yes. Request a planned visit through ServiceM8 or call, and tell us the first busy weekend you care about. We confirm the time before it is set. We serve these properties in Lee, Collier, and Charlotte County, plus LaBelle, including seasonal sites that sit quieter in the wetter months and fill in winter.

Golf courses, marinas, and campgrounds questions

Do you pump septic tanks at golf courses, marinas, and campgrounds?

Yes. Clubhouses, restrooms, marina buildings, and campground systems in our service area can be pumped when we can reach the lids. Those tanks are quoted for the system and its use. Call (239) 451-3261.

Is a campground the same as your RV park page?

They overlap, but that page is grouped with churches and schools. This page is written for golf, marina, and campground facilities, including clubhouses and dock restrooms. If you run an RV park specifically, you can use either page, or call and describe the property.

Does the $399.99 residential starting price apply?

No. That starting price is for a standard 1–3 bedroom home. Course, marina, and campground tanks are quoted.

We have a tournament or a holiday weekend coming. When should we pump?

If a pump-out is due, schedule it before the peak, not during it. Call (239) 451-3261 with the event date.
