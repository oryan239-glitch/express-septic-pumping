Septic care for landlords and Airbnb hosts

Call (239) 451-3261Book routine service

A rental or Airbnb on a septic tank has a different risk profile than a family that lives in the house all year. Guests do not know your rules, weekend groups can run a lot of water, and a backup during a stay becomes your emergency, not theirs. This page is for landlords and hosts. Property managers handling many units may prefer the managers and HOA page.

Why can a short-term rental need pumping sooner?

The EPA's general 3 to 5 year range for household tanks still applies as a starting point, but a house that sleeps more people on weekends than it was sized for will fill faster. So will a kitchen that sees vacation cooking and a bathroom where guests use wipes. Keep a pump-out date on your turnover calendar, not only in a folder you open when something fails. Residential pumping starts at $399.99 (standard 1–3 bed).

What should I put in the guest book?

Three lines are enough: do not flush wipes or paper towels, do not pour grease down the sink, and text the host if a drain is slow or the yard smells. You can link the first-time owner guide in a welcome email for long-term tenants who are new to septic. For a one-night stay, the three lines in the bathroom matter more.

How should I plan around busy season and empty weeks?

Pump before a heavy reservation stretch if the last service was a long time ago. If the house will sit for months, note the date you return or the next check-in, and do not wait until the afternoon guests arrive. That pattern is close to what snowbird owners do; the seasonal return page covers houses that go empty part of the year.

What do I do if a guest has a backup?

Ask them to stop using water. Call (239) 451-3261. After-hours pumping is $599.99. We are open 24 hours. Do not send them to the online booking form while sewage is backing up. Details are on emergency septic backup.

Landlords and short-term rentals questions

Do short-term rentals need pumping more often?

They can. Guest turnover and weekend occupancy can load a tank faster than a full-time household of the same bedroom count. Track actual use and pump on a tighter schedule if the house stays busy. Call (239) 451-3261.

What house rules help protect a septic tank in an Airbnb?

No wipes, no grease down the kitchen sink, and a note to call the host if drains slow. Put the rule where guests see it, not only in a buried listing paragraph.

What if a guest reports a backup after checkout?

Treat it as a backup: stop water use, and call us. After-hours pumping is $599.99. Online booking is for planned pump-outs, not an active backup.

Should I pump between long gaps in reservations?

If the home will sit, or if a packed season is about to start, check the last pump date and schedule service before guests arrive. See also seasonal septic care.
