Why we pump both septic tank compartments in Fort Myers, FL

Call (239) 451-3261Book routine service

A Fort Myers mailing address can be inside city utilities or on a septic tank in unincorporated Lee County, so the first step is to confirm you even have two chambers to pump. This page is for houses that still use a tank, including in South Fort Myers areas such as McGregor, Iona, Cypress Lake, and Villas, and in communities such as Tice, Fort Myers Shores, Gateway, and Buckingham when those lots still treat wastewater on site. We empty both the inlet and the outlet, and we clean the filter when one is present. Call (239) 451-3261 or request a Fort Myers-area pump-out on ServiceM8.

Start by confirming the house is on a tank

City of Fort Myers utilities and, in some streets, Lee County Utilities sewer, mean there is no septic inlet or outlet to pump. Sewer can change from one block to the next in older neighborhoods. If you do not pay a sewer bill, you likely have a tank. We will tell you whether that tank has two compartments when we open it. Houses north of the Caloosahatchee should use the North Fort Myers both-compartment page instead of this one.

Why a clogged outlet is easy to misread in an older house

In an older Fort Myers-area house, slow drains after a pump-out often get blamed on indoor plumbing or on a tank that 'must already be full again.' Sometimes the inlet was emptied and the outlet was not. Sludge and scum left in that second chamber can pack a filter or carry over toward the drain field. The house can look freshly serviced from the street. We empty both sides so that leftover load is not sitting at the last stop before the field. We do not invent a share of older-house backups that start this way; we do treat both chambers as standard work.

Finding both lids in older yards and South Fort Myers lots

Two compartments usually mean two lids. In older yards, one cover may be at grade and the other under a patio edge, a planting bed, or a driveway addition. In South Fort Myers lots the same thing happens under landscaping. Tell us if you only know one location. Locating and uncovering a buried lid is quoted on the call. That access work is how we reach the outlet chamber, not a separate 'inspection product.'

What you should ask, and what we do on the visit

Ask whether the crew will open and empty the outlet as well as the inlet, and whether they will clean the filter if there is one. We do both, and the filter cleaning is part of the pump-out, not a separate filter invoice. We can also show you how the filter seats. Fort Myers both-compartment pumping questions

How do I know if a Fort Myers address has two septic compartments?

First confirm the house is on a septic tank, not City of Fort Myers or Lee County Utilities sewer. A Fort Myers mailing address can be either. If you do not pay a sewer bill, you likely have a tank; we will empty both chambers when it is built that way. Call (239) 451-3261.

Do you empty only the inlet when you pump in Fort Myers?

No. We pump the inlet and the outlet. If an effluent filter is present, we clean it during the same visit, included with the pump-out.

Our house is older and has slow drains after a recent pump-out. Could the outlet have been skipped?

It is one possibility. A visit that empties only the first chamber can leave sludge and scum in the outlet, which can pack a filter or move toward the drain field. We open both sides and empty what is there.
