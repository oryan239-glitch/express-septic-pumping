What is distinctive about restaurant service on Marco Island?

The island's visitor economy is concentrated, and many kitchens sit close to water or in tight commercial lots. High season and holiday weekends make Collier Boulevard slower and make mid-morning midweek visits easier for a pumper truck. That is an access issue, not a price change. Which FOG program applies on the island?

City of Marco Island Water & Sewer, not Lee County Utilities, is the local sewer utility for typical city customers. The city has published grease-trap information for food-service establishments, which shows that it administers a grease program. During this research we could not independently retrieve a complete official ordinance or policy text that we could quote for specific pumping frequencies. Third-party summaries exist, but we will not copy unverified numbers onto this page. Confirm the current indoor-trap and interceptor intervals with Marco Island Water & Sewer before you set a calendar.

Do not assume Collier County's 30- and 90-day intervals automatically apply

Collier County's Collier County Food Service Establishments Program publishes 30-day indoor-trap and 90-day interceptor intervals for food-service establishments in the county program on the county wastewater system. A Marco Island restaurant on city sewer should not treat those county numbers as automatic city law. If a kitchen is actually billed by Collier County rather than the city, then the county program may apply. Check the utility bill.

What Florida law still applies?

Statewide, Florida Statute 403.0741 still sits underneath whatever interval the city sets: a signed grease waste service manifest is required, and gray-water return into the interceptor or trap is prohibited. Chapter 62-705, F.A.C., addresses handling, storage, and disposal. This Marco Island page is about pumping and scheduling, not about Express issuing manifests, holding hauler licensing, or filing reports. After you confirm the city's current interval, we can match a repeating route to it.
