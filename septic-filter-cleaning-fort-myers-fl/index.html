A Fort Myers mailing address is not a reliable guide to whether you have a septic effluent filter, because the same name covers city streets and unincorporated Lee County. Inside city limits, many homes are on City of Fort Myers utilities. Outside them, some streets are on Lee County Utilities and many houses still have a tank. Sewer service can change from one block to the next in older neighborhoods. This page is for the households that still have a tank and an outlet filter, including in South Fort Myers, Iona, and other unincorporated areas that use a Fort Myers address. We clean that filter when we pump, and we can show you how to keep it clear. Call (239) 451-3261 or request downtown or Midtown-area service on ServiceM8 and tell us the actual street.

Start by confirming you even have a tank

If you pay a sewer charge to the City of Fort Myers or to Lee County Utilities, you are not looking for this filter. If you do not, you most likely have a septic tank, as our Fort Myers septic pumping page explains. When we open the outlet, we will tell you whether a filter is present. Older tanks may not have one. Tanks that do have one need it cleaned at the pump-out and any time drains slow. We will not invent a citywide cleaning interval.

Why a clogged filter is easy to misread in an older house

In an older Fort Myers or Iona home, slow drains get blamed on aging pipes, a rain-soaked yard near the Caloosahatchee, or a tank that 'must be due.' Sometimes those are the cause. Sometimes the outlet filter is simply full, and a weekend of guests is what finally shows it. Wastewater can come back into a tub while the tank still has capacity. That is the unexpected backup this page is about. If it is already happening after hours, pumping is $699.99–$799.99 by home size; call.

Finding lids in older yards and South Fort Myers lots

We serve the city and nearby communities named on the pumping page, including South Fort Myers along McGregor, Iona, Cypress Lake, and Villas, plus Tice, Fort Myers Shores, Gateway, and Buckingham. Lids in those yards may be in a side setback, under a ship-lap fence line, or under years of grass. The filter is under the outlet lid. Locating and digging a buried lid is quoted on the call. A 6-inch riser at $299.99 each is worth asking about if you want to check the filter when the first sink slows, without waiting for another dig.

North of the river is a different page

Houses in North Fort Myers should use filter cleaning in North Fort Myers. Cape Coral has its own page because the remaining septic streets there follow a different utility story. General method is on septic filter care, and the filter service-areas hub lists the set.

What we do on a Fort Myers pump-out, besides emptying the tank

When the outlet has a filter, we clean it as part of the visit and can show you how it seats, so a later slow drain is something you can check rather than a mystery. That does not replace pumping, and it does not replace drain-field work if the field has already failed; replacement is quoted after we inspect the system. It does prevent a common kind of surprise in an older house: fixtures that fail while the tank still has room. If you just bought a South Fort Myers or Iona house and have no records, a septic inspection with pump-out is $549.99 and is a practical first look at both the tank and the filter.

Fort Myers filter cleaning questions

How do I know if a Fort Myers address has an effluent filter?

First confirm the house is on a septic tank, not City of Fort Myers or Lee County Utilities sewer. A Fort Myers mailing address can be either. If you do not pay a sewer bill, you likely have a tank; we will tell you if that tank has an outlet filter. Call (239) 451-3261.

Do you clean the filter when you pump in Fort Myers?

Yes, at no added charge, and we can show you how to keep it clear.

What does a dirty filter do in an older Fort Myers house?

It can slow or back up drains, sometimes before the tank itself is full, and send wastewater toward fixtures. That is why we clean it at the pump-out and whenever drains have already slowed.

How much is a riser in Fort Myers?

A 6-inch riser is $299.99 each. Buried-lid locating and digging is quoted on the call.
