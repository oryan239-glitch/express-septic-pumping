Golden Gate Estates is a large rural grid in eastern Collier County, and a restaurant kitchen here may sit on a septic system with an outdoor grease interceptor rather than on county sewer. This page is for food-service establishments in Golden Gate Estates, including kitchens along Immokalee Road, Golden Gate Boulevard, Everglades Boulevard, and Randall Boulevard. A 1,000-gallon grease trap or interceptor in the Estates is $695 to pump; other sizes are quoted. Call (239) 451-3261 or request an Estates visit on ServiceM8.

Where are kitchens in Golden Gate Estates?

The Estates stretch east of Naples and Golden Gate on acre-scale lots. Food service is not a dense downtown strip. It tends to sit in pockets along Immokalee Road, near Orangetree at the northern edge, and at isolated commercial sites on the big east-west and north-south boulevards. Distances between those sites are real, and a lid at the back of a large lot can take longer to reach than a plaza interceptor in North Naples. We do not name private restaurants as customers. If the kitchen is actually in closer-in Golden Gate, or in North Naples or Immokalee, use those pages and confirm the utility; the service-areas hub lists them.

Is the kitchen on Collier County sewer or on a septic tank?

Most homes in Golden Gate Estates have no central sewer and use a well and a septic tank. Some commercial sites, especially along major roads, may be connected to Collier County's wastewater system. Look at the utility bill rather than assuming either picture. If the kitchen discharges to Collier County sewer, the official Collier County Food Service Establishments Program applies: indoor grease traps must be pumped at least every 30 days, and grease interceptors at least every 90 days. Records must be kept for at least three years. That program is aimed at kitchens on the county wastewater system. It is not a blanket Estates-wide calendar for every lot on a drain field.

What if the interceptor discharges to a septic tank?

Then there is no county sewer FOG pumping ordinance to copy onto the tank. The interceptor still needs to be pumped. Grease that gets past it can overload a septic tank and move toward the drain field, which is a different and more expensive problem than a missed sewer-program date. We can set a repeating schedule based on how hard the kitchen works, not on a sewer interval that does not apply. Household septic pumping on these lots is a separate job and is covered on septic tank pumping in Golden Gate Estates. Our truck uses a split 4,200-gallon tank so grease and septic loads are not mixed. If a property has both an interceptor and a septic tank, say so when you book.

What statewide rule still applies either way?

Florida Statute 403.0741 still requires a signed grease waste service manifest with the pump-out and still prohibits putting removed grease waste back into the interceptor or trap. This Estates page is about pumping and access, not a claim that Express issues manifests, holds grease-hauler licensing, or keeps the kitchen's compliance file. After-hours pumping during an active backup is $599.99; call rather than using the form. If a lid is buried in sand or grass on a large lot, locating and digging it is $100, then $50 per additional hour.

Sources: Collier County Food Service Establishments Program (for county-sewer customers); Florida Statute 403.0741. Confirm whether the site is on county sewer or a septic tank.

Do Collier County's 30- and 90-day FOG intervals apply to every Estates kitchen?

Only if the kitchen discharges to Collier County sewer. Many Golden Gate Estates properties have no central sewer and use a septic tank with an outdoor grease interceptor. In that case there is no county sewer FOG calendar; still pump the interceptor so grease does not overload the tank. Confirm the utility, if any, on the bill.

Does Florida Statute 403.0741 still apply on a septic lot?

Yes. The statute still requires a signed grease waste service manifest for the pump-out and still prohibits returning removed grease waste to the unit. This page does not present Express as the kitchen's manifest or reporting office.
