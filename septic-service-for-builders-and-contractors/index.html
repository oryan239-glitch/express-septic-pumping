If you are building, remodeling, or running a crew on a lot that already has a septic tank, the drain field is the part of the yard that cannot be treated like extra staging space. This page is for general contractors, excavation crews, and trade partners who need the tank pumped or need to keep the field intact while other work happens. Express pumps tanks; we quote other system work only after we inspect.

How do I protect the drain field during construction?

Find out where the tank and drain field are before the first delivery of fill or equipment. Do not park trucks, store block, or cut a temporary road across the field. Compacted soil and broken laterals are much harder to undo than a marked-off corner of the lot. If you are not sure where the field is, stop and have it located rather than guessing with a skid steer.

When should a job site call for pumping?

Call when a temporary facility is on the existing tank, when a house has been occupied through a remodel, or when the tank must be opened before other trades close up the yard. Tell us about mud, fences, and overhead work so we can reach the lids. Buried-lid locating and digging is $100, plus $50 for each additional hour.

How do new-construction and remodel access differ?

On a remodel, the tank is often already in a finished yard, sometimes with landscaping over the lids. On an open pad, the risk is equipment traffic more than hidden lids. In both cases we need a clear path for the hose and a contact who knows the lot. We pump both the inlet and outlet compartments when we pump, and we clean an effluent filter at no charge if the tank has one.

Can you coordinate with the plumber or the owner?

Yes. Have the site supervisor or the property owner call (239) 451-3261 with the address and the preferred window. Routine visits can also be requested through ServiceM8. If a drain field is already failing, do not assume a pump-out will finish the project; drain field work is quoted after inspection.

Builders and contractors questions

Can crews drive or stage materials on a drain field?

No. Parking, storing fill, or running equipment over the field can compact soil and damage pipes. Mark the field before work starts and keep traffic off it.

Do you pump tanks on active job sites?

Yes, when we can reach the lids safely. Call (239) 451-3261 with access notes, gate codes, and where the tank is relative to the work.

Is construction pumping priced like a house pump-out?

Residential pumping starts at $399.99 (standard 1–3 bed). Work tied to a construction site is discussed when we see access and tank size. Drain field and repair work are quoted after inspection.

Who should be on site when you arrive?

Someone who can show lid locations, unlock gates, and confirm buried utilities in the work area.
