Why we pump both septic tank compartments in Port Charlotte, FL

Call (239) 451-3261Book routine service

Port Charlotte sits in Charlotte County, north of the Peace River from Punta Gorda, and many houses on the canal grid, around Murdock, and along Tamiami Trail still use a septic tank, which means a finished visit still covers both the inlet and the outlet, not only the first lid on a canal street. Some sites may instead be on Charlotte County Utilities sewer; check the bill before you ask about two chambers. This page is for the lots that still have a tank. Call (239) 451-3261 or request a Port Charlotte pump-out on ServiceM8.

Canal streets and the Murdock area still need both compartments emptied

A canal-front lot can have the tank close to the water and a tight side yard. A Murdock-area or inland lot may hide the covers farther from the street. On either kind of lot, wastewater still hits the inlet first. Solids and scum should stay in that first chamber, and the outlet should send clearer water on to the drain field. If only the inlet is pumped, leftover solids can travel toward the field or load a filter until fixtures slow. We clear the inlet and the outlet on a two-chamber tank, and we clean the outlet filter when one is there, included in the pump-out.

Seasonal streets fill up; the method does not get a special calendar

When winter residents return, a house that sat quiet can load the tank quickly. That is a reason to book a full pump-out, not a reason to skip a chamber or to invent a required month that is supposed to fit every seasonal Port Charlotte street. We will not publish a numbered interval here. When we pump, we empty the inlet and the outlet.

This is not Punta Gorda, and the bill still has to be checked

Punta Gorda is the county seat south of the Peace River, with a different mix of city utilities and tanks. Do not use that page by habit for a Port Charlotte address. And do not assume every lot here is still on septic; some may be billed by Charlotte County Utilities for sewer and would not have chambers to empty. If the bill shows a sewer charge, this page does not apply.

Access on a canal lot, and what locating a lid costs

Tell us if the tank sits near a canal, under plantings, or a long way from the driveway so we can plan to reach both covers in one stop. A buried lid that has to be located and dug starts at $100, with $50 for each extra hour. Charlotte County and the other city pages are listed on the why we pump both tanks hub. Port Charlotte both-compartment pumping questions

Are Port Charlotte houses usually on septic?

Many homes in Port Charlotte are still on septic, including lots on the canal grid and in the Murdock area. Some sites may instead be billed by Charlotte County Utilities for sewer. Check the bill before you schedule a pump-out. Call (239) 451-3261 if you want help reading it.

If the lot is on a tank, do you pump both compartments?

Yes, when the tank is built with two chambers. A filter in the outlet, when there is one, gets cleaned on that visit; there is no added filter line item.

Does seasonal occupancy change which chamber you empty?

It can change how quickly the tank fills when winter residents return. It does not change the work. We still empty the inlet and the outlet. We will not publish a required month that is supposed to fit every seasonal street.
