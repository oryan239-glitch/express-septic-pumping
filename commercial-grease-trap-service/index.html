Commercial grease trap service

Call Express (239) 451-3261Request service

Restaurants and commercial kitchens need their grease traps pumped before fats, oils and grease build up and cause backups, odors or problems with the drain line. Express pumps grease traps for businesses across Southwest Florida.

Our 4,200-gallon split-tank truck keeps grease separate from septic loads, so we can handle grease trap work and septic work on the same truck.

One-time or recurring

Some businesses call us when a trap is full. Most are better off on a recurring schedule based on how much cooking they do and the size of the trap. Tell us about your kitchen and we'll talk through how often makes sense.

Ask about recurring service

Signs your grease trap needs service

  • Slow sinks or floor drains in the kitchen
  • Grease or odors around the trap lid
  • It's been longer than your usual schedule since the last pump-out

What to have ready when you call

  • Business name and address
  • Where the trap is and how it's accessed
  • Trap size, if you know it
  • When it was last pumped
