This is a short overview. Our main page for restaurants and food service is grease trap and interceptor pumping, covering in-ground interceptors and indoor traps in Fort Myers, Cape Coral, Bonita Springs, Naples, Estero and across Lee, Collier and Charlotte County, plus LaBelle.

Restaurants and commercial kitchens need their grease traps pumped before fats, oils and grease build up and cause backups, odors or problems with the drain line. Express can set up a one-time pump-out or a recurring schedule. $695 for a 1,000-gallon grease trap or interceptor; other sizes are quoted.

Lee County interceptor pumping rule

Lee County Ordinance 14-24 requires grease interceptors to be fully pumped at least every 90 calendar days. Indoor grease traps must be cleaned at least weekly or whenever grease and solids exceed 20% of the unit volume. We can set up a recurring pump-out schedule. Details are on the grease trap and interceptor pumping page.

Our 4,200-gallon truck has a split tank, one side for septic and one side for grease, so grease trap loads and septic loads never mix.

One-time or recurring

Some businesses call us when a trap is full. Most are better off on a recurring schedule based on how much cooking they do and the size of the trap. Tell us about your kitchen and we'll talk through how often makes sense.

Signs your grease trap needs service

  • Slow sinks or floor drains in the kitchen
  • Grease or odors around the trap lid
  • It's been longer than your usual schedule since the last pump-out

What to have ready when you call

  • Business name and address
  • Where the trap is and how it's accessed
  • Trap size, if you know it
  • When it was last pumped
