What to do when a drain field is not draining

Call (239) 451-3261Book routine service

Open 24 hours, 7 days · Owner-operated from Estero · Dispatch confirms every appointment by phone or text.

If wastewater is pooling over the drain field, the grass there is suddenly lush and wet, or drains in the house are slow at the same time, stop adding water and call. This page is about that problem, not a general maintenance overview. A wet field in Southwest Florida can mean a full tank, a dirty filter, a high water table after rain, or a field that is no longer taking water. Those are different problems, and they are not diagnosed from a photo alone.

What are the signs the field is not taking water?

Standing water or persistently soggy soil over the drain field, sewage odor in the yard, extra-green grass in that footprint, and slow or backing-up fixtures inside the house can show up together. After a heavy rain, some yards are wet everywhere; a field problem is more likely when the wet or smelly area sits over the drain field and stays after the rest of the lot dries.

What should I do before anyone arrives?

Stop laundry, long showers, and dishwasher runs. Do not drive or park on the wet area. Do not trench, add gravel, or 'air out' the field with a homemade fix. Call (239) 451-3261. If sewage is already backing up inside, treat it as a backup and call day or night. After-hours pumping is starting at $599.99.

Can pumping the tank help a field that looks failed?

Sometimes. If the tank is full, sludge is escaping, or the effluent filter is clogged, emptying the tank and cleaning the filter can take pressure off the field. Express pumps both compartments and cleans the filter on pump-outs. If the field itself has failed, a pump-out will not make the soil work like new. Drain field replacement is quoted after we inspect the system.

Why is this more common in the rainy season here?

Sandy soils and a high water table leave less unsaturated soil under many Southwest Florida yards when summer storms sit over the area. A field that was marginal in dry weather can start to show at the surface in those weeks. That is a reason to pump when you are due, not a reason to assume every wet yard needs a new field. If you are trying to read a lush or soggy footprint before it becomes a pooling failure, see wet spots and green grass over the drain field. For the June through September pattern, see rainy season septic care.

Drain field not draining questions

Does a soggy drain field always mean the field has failed?

No. A full tank, a dirty outlet filter, or a high water table after heavy rain can also leave the yard wet. We start by looking at the tank. Drain field replacement is quoted only after we inspect the system.

What should I do today if the yard is soggy over the field?

Cut back water use, do not run extra laundry, and call (239) 451-3261. Do not drive on the wet area and do not add fill or more pipe on your own.

Will a pump-out fix a drain field that is not draining?

It can help if the tank is full and solids or a clogged filter are part of the problem. It will not rebuild a field that has already failed. We will tell you what we see.

When is replacement even on the table?

Only after an inspection. We do not quote drain field replacement over the phone from a description of wet grass.
