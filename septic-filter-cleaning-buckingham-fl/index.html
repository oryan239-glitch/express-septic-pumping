Acreage lots put the lid farther away than a city yard

The screen lives beneath the outlet cover, at the discharge end of the tank. On a Buckingham parcel that lid may be a long walk from the driveway, past a gate or a stand of trees, rather than beside a short suburban walkway. Tell us about access when you book so the hose run is planned for an acreage lot. If the cover is buried and we have to find and expose it, the charge is $100 and then $50 an hour beyond that. Plan on $299.99 for each 6-inch riser, which is often the practical upgrade on a large lot: the next filter check starts at grade instead of with another dig.

Why a neglected outlet is an expensive risk on a large-lot field

The drain field on an acreage property is still the part of the system that is most expensive to replace. The filter's job is to keep solids in the tank. If it clogs, wastewater can back toward fixtures while the tank may still have room left. If it is missing or damaged, solids can move toward that field. Cleaning the filter will not replace a field that has already failed, and it will not replace pumping. It will remove a bottleneck you can actually do something about between larger repairs. We do not invent a count of Buckingham fields that failed because of a dirty filter.

Year-round households and houses that sit quieter for part of the year can put different loads on the same size tank; that is a reason to watch the fixtures, not a reason for us to invent a single interval.
