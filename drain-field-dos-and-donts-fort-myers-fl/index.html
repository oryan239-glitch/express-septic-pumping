In Fort Myers, the first drain field question is whether the house still has one: many streets are on city sewer, and this page is only for properties whose wastewater is still treated on the lot, including older houses and South Fort Myers yards. Check the bill before you look for laterals. If a tank and a field are still in service, daily habits in the kitchen, the bathroom, and the driveway all reach that soil. Call (239) 451-3261 or request a Fort Myers pump-out on ServiceM8.

Confirm the tank, then keep grease out of the lines

A house on City of Fort Myers sewer has no household field to protect. A house that still uses a tank should keep cooking oil and fats out of the sink. They do not 'wash through' an older plumbing run; they settle in the line and can travel toward the laterals. We do not invent a local failure statistic. We do treat kitchen grease as off-limits on every Fort Myers lot that still has laterals.

What should an older Fort Myers bathroom never flush?

Wipes, including those sold as flushable, go in the wastebasket, not the toilet. Biodegradable, septic-safe paper is the roll we recommend in an older Fort Myers bath. In an older house it can be tempting to treat the toilet as the easy bin; that habit is hard on the tank and on the field it feeds.

Cleaners, bacteria, and a field in an older yard

Normal household cleaning in an older Fort Myers bath is not the target. Pouring leftover bleach or a harsh cleaner down a drain is, because those products can interrupt the organisms that treat waste in the tank and in the soil. See good bacteria, the tank, and the field.

Driveways, trees, and add-on structures in older and South Fort Myers lots

An older yard may have gained a parking pad, a shed, or a shade tree after the field was installed. Do not park or drive on the laterals. Do not add a pool, a spa, or a building on that footprint. Keep deep roots off the field; turf is the right cover on an older Fort Myers lot. Aim downspouts and sprinklers away from that strip so a July storm is not sitting on soil that already has laterals under it.

Laundry, disposals, and staying on a pumping schedule

Space washing through the week. A disposal adds solids, so it is a reason to stay current on pumping. Restaurant rules in the city, versus this household page

Kitchens on City of Fort Myers sewer follow the city's pretreatment program; confirm the current pumping schedule with the city rather than inventing a number here. Lee County Ordinance 14-24 applies to food-service establishments discharging to Lee County Utilities. Interceptors on that system must be fully emptied at least every 90 calendar days. Indoor traps under the same ordinance are cleaned at least weekly, or sooner if grease and solids exceed 20% of the unit volume. Do not attach the 20% rule to interceptors, and do not use this page as a stand-in for a North Fort Myers kitchen. Fort Myers drain field do's and don'ts questions

How do I know if my Fort Myers house still has a drain field?

Check the utility bill. A City of Fort Myers sewer charge means this habit page does not apply. If wastewater stays on the lot, the do's and don'ts here still matter. Call (239) 451-3261.

Is this the right page for a house in North Fort Myers?

No. North Fort Myers is unincorporated, north of the river, and has its own habit page. Use the page that matches the address.

What should I use in the bathroom, and what should I throw away?

Use biodegradable, septic-safe toilet paper. Keep wet wipes, even flushable labels, out of the toilet.
