On a Buckingham acreage lot east of Fort Myers, the drain field often has room to spread, but that is not a reason to leave the outlet chamber full; leftover solids still travel toward a field that is expensive to repair, and the second lid is often farther from the house than it would be in a city yard. Homes here typically use a septic tank. We empty both compartments when the tank is built that way. Call (239) 451-3261 or request a Buckingham pump-out on ServiceM8.

A large lot does not make a one-sided pump-out less of a problem

Wastewater enters the inlet first. Solids settle, scum floats, and a baffle is supposed to keep most of that load in the first chamber. The outlet sits just ahead of the field, and often just ahead of an effluent filter. If a hose only works the lid nearest the driveway, sludge and scum can remain in the second chamber and then move into the field or pack the filter until fixtures slow. A wider yard does not absorb that leftover load. We empty both sides, and we clean the outlet filter when one is there, included in the pump-out.

Long driveways and gates are why we plan for two covers

Many Buckingham tanks sit well back from the road, sometimes past a gate. The second cover can be even farther from the parking spot than the first. Mention the driveway, a gate code, or a tank that sits away from the house when you schedule, so the farther lid is not left behind. If a cover has to be found and exposed, locating and digging starts at $100, then $50 for each extra hour.
