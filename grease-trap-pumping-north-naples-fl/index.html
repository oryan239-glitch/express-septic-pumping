What does Collier County require for county sewer customers?

The official Collier County Food Service Establishments Program is the primary source for this area when the kitchen discharges to Collier County's wastewater system. Indoor grease traps must be pumped at least every 30 days. Grease interceptors must be pumped at least every 90 days. Records must be kept for at least three years and must include the service date, hauler name, disposal site, and volume or condition pumped. The county does not maintain a list of approved haulers. What does Florida Statute 403.0741 require?

The statute requires that a signed grease waste service manifest accompany the pump-out and that removed grease waste not be put back into the unit. We pump the interceptor or trap and can align a repeating visit with the 30-day or 90-day interval that applies to the equipment. We do not offer to keep the kitchen's three-year file, issue manifests, or serve as a licensed grease hauler.
