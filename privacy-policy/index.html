1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy describes how Express Septic Tank Pumping LLC ("Express Septic Tank Pumping," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects personal information when you visit our website at https://expresssepticpumping.com/, call us, text us, submit a form, or otherwise use our services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact information: name, mobile and other phone numbers, email address, and service or billing address.
  • Service information: property details, septic system details, service requests, appointment dates and times, job notes, and service history.
  • Communications: records of phone calls, text messages, emails, and website form submissions you send to us, including your SMS opt-in and opt-out status.
  • Payment information: information needed to invoice and collect payment. Card payments are processed by third-party payment processors; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Website information: basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, and referring pages, collected through standard server logs and cookies.

We collect this information directly from you (by phone, text, email, or our website contact form) and automatically when you use our website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide quotes;
  • Schedule, confirm, and remind you of service appointments;
  • Follow up on missed calls and service requests;
  • Provide customer service and answer your questions;
  • Perform, invoice, and collect payment for services;
  • Send service-related text messages to customers who have opted in (see Section 5);
  • Maintain business records, comply with legal obligations, and protect against fraud or misuse; and
  • Improve our website and services.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. How We Share Your Information

We share personal information only as needed to run our business, and only with:

  • Service providers that perform services on our behalf, such as our telephone and messaging provider, scheduling/invoicing software, payment processors, email providers, and website hosting. These providers may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • Legal and safety purposes: when required by law, subpoena, or court order, or to protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our customers or others.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, sale, or transfer of all or part of our business, subject to this Privacy Policy.

SMS consent is not shared with third parties. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Information sharing with subcontractors in support of services, such as customer service and message delivery, is permitted, but all of the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

5. Text Messaging (SMS)

Express Septic Tank Pumping LLC is the business that sends text messages under this program. Messages about booking and service are sent from (239) 451-3261.

Information collected in connection with SMS: name, mobile phone number, email address, service or billing address, and service details needed to respond to your booking or service request.

Message types: booking replies when you text BOOK, appointment reminders, scheduling confirmations, missed-call follow-ups, and customer service replies related to septic tank pumping, grease trap pumping, and related local services.

How you opt in: You may opt in by (a) texting BOOK to (239) 451-3261 to get our online booking link and receive related booking messages; (b) verbally agreeing to receive text messages during a phone call with us when you provide your mobile number or request to be contacted by text; or (c) checking the optional SMS consent box (unchecked by default) on the quote/request form on our website, agreeing to receive text messages from Express Septic Tank Pumping LLC at the number provided about your quote, appointment scheduling, arrival times, and service updates. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

Key terms:

  • Message frequency varies.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • To opt out at any time, reply STOP. You will receive one confirmation message and no further messages unless you opt in again.
  • For help, reply HELP or call (239) 451-3261.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in consent are never shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Service providers that deliver messages on our behalf may process that data only to send those messages for us.
