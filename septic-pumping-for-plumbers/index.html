Septic pumping for plumbers

Call (239) 451-3261Book routine service

Plumbers often need the septic tank pumped or opened before they can finish a repair, find a blockage, or rule the tank in or out of a slow-drain complaint. This page is for licensed plumbers and their dispatchers who want a straightforward way to get that truck on site. We pump tanks; we do not take over the plumbing scope, and we are not asking you to give up your customer. Call (239) 451-3261 with the address, your contact, and whether the house is already overflowing.

When is a pump-out the missing step in a plumbing call?

When several fixtures are slow, when a toilet gurgles as another drain is used, when the lowest shower backs up, or when you suspect a full tank or a clogged outlet filter rather than a single-line clog. Cabling one fixture will not empty the outlet compartment. A planned residential pump-out starts at $399.99 for a standard 1–3 bedroom home. If the tank has a filter, we clean it on the pump-out, which is often the detail that made the house act 'stopped up.' For the homeowner-facing version of that pattern, see gurgling and slow drains.

How should dispatch coordinate the visit?

Tell us who the customer is, who will be on site, where the lids are if you already uncovered them, and any deadline you are working against. Routine, non-overflow work can be requested through ServiceM8; we confirm the time. If you already have a lid open, that saves locating time. If the lid is still buried, locating and digging is quoted on the call. We will pump both compartments. After-hours pumping is $699.99–$799.99 by home size when the call is an overflow outside routine hours.

What is the working relationship on the job?

We arrive as Express Septic Tank Pumping, do the pumping work, and tell you and the owner what we saw. If your company is arranging the visit, be clear about who we should bill and who can authorize extra access work. We do not publish a subcontract price list or a private plumber tariff on this page. We also do not repair interior piping, replace a house water softener, or quote a new drain field from a phone description. Drain field and repair work on the septic system, if needed, is quoted after we inspect.

What should the homeowner hear from both of us?

That a pump-out is not a house-plumbing guarantee, and that a plumbing repair is not a substitute for a full tank. If the field is wet or the yard smells, the owner can read wet spots and green grass or drain field not draining while we are on the way. If you are also the contractor remodeling the bath, keep the new finishes off the drain-field footprint; builders and contractors covers that protection. Construction-site access is on construction sites.

What if you only need us after hours?

Call. We are open 24 hours, 7 days a week for this kind of request. After-hours pumping is $699.99–$799.99 by home size. The occupant should stop running water until we arrive. We serve plumbers and their customers in Lee, Collier, and Charlotte County, plus LaBelle, from our Estero shop at 23357 Olde Meadowbrook Circle.

Pumping for plumbers questions

Can a plumber call you to pump a tank so their work can proceed?

Yes. Call (239) 451-3261 with the address, the plumber's contact, and what you need opened or emptied. Routine visits can also be requested on ServiceM8.

Do you work as a subcontractor under our company name?

We pump the tank as Express. Billing, access, and who the customer is should be clear before we roll. We do not invent a formal franchise or co-branded program on this page.

What if the house is already backing up when you call us?

The occupant should stop running water, and someone should call (239) 451-3261. After-hours pumping is $699.99–$799.99 by home size. Do not use the online booking form during an overflow.

Will you repair the drain field or the plumbing in the house?

We pump tanks and quote other system work after we inspect. Interior drain lines and fixture repairs remain the plumber's trade unless a separate scope is agreed after we look at the system.
