Property managers and HOA boards are usually the ones who get the call when a tenant or a resident has a slow drain on a Friday night. This page is about keeping pumping dates, lid locations, and after-hours contacts in one place so you are not starting from zero at each turnover. How should I track septic service across multiple addresses?

Keep a simple log: address, tank access notes, last pump-out date, and whether a riser is already in place. When a board or owner changes, that log is what you hand off. If dates are missing, treat the tank as overdue and schedule a visit rather than guessing. Residential pumping starts at $399.99 (standard 1–3 bed). An inspection with pump-out is $549.99 when the history is a blank page.

What belongs in a tenant or resident handout?

A short list works better than a manual: no wipes (including flushable labels), no grease, no paint or medications down the toilet, and a phone number to call if drains gurgle or the yard is soggy. Point people who want more detail to the first-time septic guide. You do not need to teach soil bacteria on move-in day; you need fewer emergency calls.

How do after-hours backups work for a managed property?

Tell occupants to stop running water and to call you or (239) 451-3261 directly. Online booking is for routine work, not an active backup. We are open 24 hours, 7 days a week. After-hours pumping is $699.99–$799.99 by home size. More detail is on the emergency backup page.

Can an HOA set a neighborhood-wide pumping reminder?

Yes, as a policy or a reminder letter, even if each lot is a separate system. A shared calendar for older sections of a community, especially where lids are buried, reduces the number of 'we cannot find the tank' visits. Locating and digging a buried lid is quoted on the call. A riser at $299.99 each is often the longer-term fix for those lots.

Property managers and HOAs questions

Can you put several properties on a recurring pump-out schedule?

Yes. Call (239) 451-3261 with the addresses and last service dates, and we can set a schedule that you track for the board or owner.

What should tenants be told about the septic system?

A short house rule: no wipes, grease, or paint down the drains, and call management immediately if drains slow or an odor appears. Our first-time guide can be shared as background.

What does after-hours service cost if a unit backs up at night?

After-hours pumping is $699.99–$799.99 by home size. For a backup, the occupant should stop running water and call us or you should call on their behalf. Do not use the online booking form for an active backup.

Do HOAs need anything different than a single rental house?

The plumbing is the same idea, but you may be coordinating common-area systems or many lots. Keep a shared log of pump dates and lid locations so a turnover in the board does not lose the map.
