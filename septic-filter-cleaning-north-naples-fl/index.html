Why a dirty filter and a high water table are a bad pair

Our North Naples septic pumping page already notes that high water tables in the rainy season put extra stress on drain fields. The effluent filter does not lower the water table. What it does is keep solids in the tank so the field is asked to treat liquid, not sludge. If the filter is packed, wastewater can back up into the house instead of leaving the tank. If it is missing or bypassed, solids can move toward a field that already has less unsaturated soil in September than it did in March. We will not invent a depth to water or a failure rate. We will say that cleaning the filter is one of the few steps you control before the wet months arrive.

When should a North Naples filter be cleaned?

At every pump-out, and any time drains slow. That is the whole published rule. Seasonal residents who arrive in winter should not wait on a drain that is already sluggish; a filter that sat all summer can be part of the problem, and so can a tank that was not pumped before the house filled up. We will look at both. After-hours pumping during an active backup is $599.99; use the phone.

Access on larger North Naples lots

Tanks on wider lots can sit a long hose run from the driveway, and lids may be in a lawn between the house and a preserve line or a swale. The filter is under the outlet lid. If we have to locate and dig, the charge is $100, plus $50 for each additional hour. A 6-inch riser costs $299.99 each and makes later filter checks possible without digging, which matters if you want to look at it when the first slow drain appears rather than waiting for the next full pump-out.

What we do on the visit, and what we do not claim

We pump the tank, clean the filter as part of that visit, and can show you how it seats in the outlet. We do not sell a separate invented filter-only price on this page, and we do not treat filter cleaning as a substitute for pumping or for drain-field work if the field has already failed. North Naples filter cleaning questions

Why is filter cleaning a bigger deal in North Naples during the rainy season?

A high water table already stresses a drain field. A clogged outlet filter adds a second bottleneck and can push wastewater back toward the house. Clean the filter at every pump-out and whenever drains slow. Call (239) 451-3261.

Do you clean the filter at no charge in North Naples?

Yes, when we pump the tank. We can also show you the outlet lid and how the filter is seated.

How much are risers and buried-lid digging?

A 6-inch riser is $299.99 each. Locating and digging a buried lid is $100, plus $50 for each additional hour.

We are only here part of the year. Should we have the filter checked when we arrive?

If drains are slow when you open the house, yes. Otherwise have it cleaned at the pump-out you schedule. There is no single month we assign to every seasonal household.
