Express Septic Tank Pumping

Commercial septic pumping for hotels, motels, and short-term rentals

Call (239) 451-3261Book routine service

Hotels, motels, and professionally operated short-term rental properties put a commercial occupancy pattern on a septic tank: rooms turn over, laundry runs in waves, and a backup becomes a guest-service problem in a hurry. This page is that commercial lodging angle. Individual hosts and small Airbnb operators should use septic care for landlords and short-term rentals, which is written for house rules and guest books. Property managers of many residential lots may prefer property managers and HOAs. Call (239) 451-3261 to talk about a hotel, motel, or professionally run rental building.

How is lodging use different from a household rental?

A family house that is occasionally listed online still behaves like a house. A motel, a small hotel, or a professionally staffed rental compound behaves like a business: peak check-in days, housekeeping carts, commercial laundry, and sometimes a breakfast room or a bar. That load is not well described by the EPA's 3 to 5 year household range alone. A planned, recurring pump-out around occupancy is the more practical approach. Commercial tanks are quoted for the system and its use. The published residential starting price of $399.99 is for a standard 1–3 bedroom home and does not automatically apply here.

What about laundry rooms and on-site food service?

A laundry room can send a large volume of water to the tank in a short window, especially on turnover days. If you can stagger loads, do it; if the tank is due, pump before a busy weekend rather than during it. A kitchen, breakfast line, or pool snack bar may also have a grease trap or interceptor, which is a separate service from septic pumping. A 1,000-gallon grease unit is $695; other sizes are quoted. Do not assume a grease pump-out will correct a septic backup in the guest rooms, or the reverse. See grease trap and interceptor pumping for those units.

How should a general manager plan access?

Give us a maintenance or night-audit contact, a place to stage the truck that does not block registrations, and notes about lids in courtyards, behind fences, or under landscaping. Locating and digging a buried lid is $100, plus $50 for each additional hour. A riser at $299.99 each is often worth it on a property that will be on a regular route. Routine work can be requested through ServiceM8; we confirm the appointment. If several buildings share a system, tell us that up front rather than discovering a second tank in the dark.

What should night staff do when a room or a hallway backs up?

Stop water use on the affected fixtures, keep guests and pets away from spilled sewage, and call (239) 451-3261. After-hours pumping is $599.99. Do not send the desk clerk to the online booking form while sewage is overflowing. We pump the tank, including both compartments, and clean an effluent filter at no charge if one is present. We will tell you what we saw. Restoration of carpets or walls is not our trade; insurance and restoration contacts can start with insurance and restoration companies if that is the next call you need to make.

When should we pump relative to high season?

If a pump-out is due, schedule it before the busiest occupancy stretch, not after the first slow drain on a Saturday. Southwest Florida's winter season and holiday weeks are the obvious peaks; summer storms can also stress a field on a canal or a low lot. For rain-related backups see backup after heavy rain. Drain field work, if it is ever on the table, is quoted after we inspect the system. We serve lodging properties across Lee, Collier, and Charlotte County, plus LaBelle.

Hotels, motels, and commercial rentals questions

Do you pump septic tanks at hotels and motels?

Yes. Commercial lodging tanks are quoted for the system and its use. Call (239) 451-3261 with the property, access notes, and a night or maintenance contact.

Is this the same as your Airbnb host page?

No. The landlords and short-term rentals page is written for individual hosts and small rentals. This page is the commercial lodging and professionally operated rental-property angle.

Does a hotel use the $399.99 residential starting price?

No. That published starting price is for a standard 1–3 bedroom home. Hotel, motel, and other commercial tanks are quoted.

A guest floor is backing up at 2 a.m. What should we do?

Stop water use on the affected fixtures, keep guests away from spilled sewage, and call (239) 451-3261. After-hours pumping is $599.99. Do not use the online booking form during an overflow.
